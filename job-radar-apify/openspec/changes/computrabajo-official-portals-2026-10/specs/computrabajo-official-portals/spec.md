# Computrabajo official portal coverage

- The target census MUST include live Colombia jobs when `Computrabajo` is either the primary source or a deduplicated source.
- The census MUST exclude official projections, undisclosed employer names and Venezuela-only jobs.
- The report MUST distinguish a linked enabled official board from a board with a currently live official posting, and MUST NOT imply one-to-one replacement of Computrabajo jobs.
- The production census MUST be read-only, bounded, and manually dispatched.
- The full target backlog MUST be exportable from a manual read-only run. Manual official-site review status MUST remain distinct from automated ATS name-probe decisions and be linked to evidence.
- Scheduled discovery MUST prioritize live Computrabajo Colombia companies by their Computrabajo job count. A manual all-sources option MUST remain available.
- Official board promotion MUST continue to require evidence from the employer's own site or the existing ATS verification rules; a company name or candidate domain alone MUST NOT authorize promotion.
- Blocked, absent and incomplete listings MUST NOT be interpreted as closed jobs.
