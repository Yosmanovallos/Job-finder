# City hub pages: query wording and crawlable city network

Phase 4 of `docs/SEO-GROWTH-EXECUTION-PLAN.md`. Evidence:
`docs/seo-baseline/2026-10-24-measurement-baseline.md` (GSC 90 days).
City pages collect impressions at positions 17–33 (Caracas 350 / 28.6,
Santa Marta 260 / 22.8, Neiva 165 / 16.7, Valledupar 90 / 26.5). The
dominant query variants are "empleos <ciudad>", "trabajo(s) en <ciudad>"
and "ofertas de empleo en <ciudad>"; titles only said "Trabajo en …:
vacantes de empleo". Pages had no H2 and no links between city hubs.
"trabajos remotos" ranks at position 3.0 with zero clicks, while the
remote bucket rendered as "Trabajo en Remoto".

Scope: title/H1 wording for city pages and the remote bucket, two H2
sections, and links to the other real city pages of the same country.
No new URLs, no count changes, no job data inferred, no query changes.
Role pages keep their title and get only the jobs H2.
Not in scope: ordering by readiness, pagination beyond 60 rows.
